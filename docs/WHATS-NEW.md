- Smoother playing on phones: loading a big playlist or TV guide now waits its turn behind the channel you're watching, and the animated Wave and Cross backgrounds are no longer drawn behind the video.
- Films and series: on phones the top and bottom player controls now hide together, and using the top row keeps both up. Settings › Playback can now show you the list of streams instead of playing (it fills in as add-ons answer), skips a stream that hasn't started in 15 seconds, leaves very big files until last, and can send films and series to another player app like VLC. New Subtitle timing button in the player moves subtitles earlier or later. Thanks to u/Turbulent-Invite2673 on Reddit.
- Fixed: in the Glass, Wave, Fluent, Cross and Spotlight looks (and Broadcast and Classic), main buttons like Update now had white writing on a white or yellow button. They now use dark writing. Switches and the country chooser are fixed the same way.
- Live TV can now group channels by topic (News, Sport, Kids…) with the new Groups button, sort the best-known channels of your countries first (Order › Importance, countries in Settings › Channels), and show a channel's regional and +1 versions as one line.
- Fixed on TV: pressing Update now in Settings › Updates keeps you on the download instead of jumping back to the top of Settings. Thanks to u/CrazyDowntown5881 on Reddit for lots of suggestions and bug reports, now in Help and about › Credits.
- Fixed: the picture size and zoom buttons now change the picture straight away, and the full-screen player on phones now fills the notch area too.
- Sports add-ons that list their rows as "sports" or "events" now show on Sports TV, and protected (ClearKey) streams from add-ons now play, so sports add-ons' encrypted sources work too.
- Add a Jellyfin server in Sources (username and password, or Quick Connect): its films and series appear on Movies and TV Series, and its Live TV and guide in Live TV. Add-ons with "other" rows (mixed collections) now show them on Movies and TV Series too.
