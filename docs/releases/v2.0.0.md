VantaView 2.0 is a big update: films and series from your playlist, eight languages, torrents, Sports TV, a phone-friendly setup and much more.

**Download:** https://github.com/wardy-uk1/VantaView/releases/latest/download/VantaView.apk (always the newest version)

What's new:

- **Your playlist's films and series** (M3U and Xtream Codes) now show on Movies and TV Series, with posters and details, and a country or language tag (like DE) from your provider's groups.
- **Eight new languages:** Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Dutch, Polish and Turkish. VantaView follows your device, or pick one per profile in Settings › Appearance. "What's new" is translated too.
- **Add a playlist from your phone:** on Sources or during setup, choose Show QR code, scan it, and paste your provider's message, an M3U link or file, or Xtream Codes on your phone. It goes straight to the TV over your home Wi-Fi.
- **Playlists page:** see each playlist's server and username, switch playlists off and on, and new playlists are named after their server.
- **Live TV:** preview a channel in a small window before going full screen; channel up and down follow the list you came from (Favourites included); a Catch-up tile on Home.
- **Sports TV:** add-ons with live channels and events get their own page, with Open in browser for add-ons that only link to web pages. Add-on set-up page addresses work as install links.
- **Play torrents** with the built-in player when there's no debrid account (debrid is still tried first). It only shares while you watch, and the file is deleted when you stop.
- **Speed test** with ping, download and upload on a live dial.
- **Easier on a TV:** settings sections are drop-downs, the remote moves straight past text boxes, the keyboard only opens when you press OK, and a Screen size setting fits the app to your screen.
- **More:** Refresh on Home, picture-in-picture on/off, Stalker (MAG) portals, Trakt sync per profile, Skip intro and Next episode, console-style looks, Catch-up page, trailers and cast, Nuvio backup restore, the version and build on the start-up screen, an update check every time VantaView starts, and VantaView reopening itself after an update.
- **Privacy:** M3U playlist links are now stored encrypted. See docs/PRIVACY.md for exactly where your logins go.

Installs over 1.0.1 with no need to uninstall.
