VantaView 2.1.0 adds Jellyfin servers, Stremio sign-in, more picture sizes and a mode for 1 GB TVs.

**Download:** https://github.com/wardy-uk1/VantaView/releases/latest/download/VantaView.apk (always the newest version)

What's new:

- **Jellyfin servers:** add one in Sources (username and password, or Quick Connect). Its films and series go to Movies and TV Series, and its Live TV and guide to Live TV.
- **Stremio sign-in:** Settings › Stremio, with a QR code or your email. Add-ons, Library, Continue watching and watched films stay in step with your Stremio account, both ways.
- **Picture sizes:** Fit, Stretch, Fill screen, 16:9, 4:3, 21:9 and Original, plus Zoom up to 200% that can hold the middle, top or bottom. Each channel, film and series remembers its own.
- **Full screen covers the notch** on phones, and stays full screen when one channel is opened from another.
- **Low-memory mode for 1 GB TVs:** turns itself on (Settings › Appearance › Memory), with lighter effects, smaller artwork and a smaller buffer. Big provider lists no longer have to fit in memory at once.
- **Smart Add from a screenshot:** choose a picture of your provider's message (or send one from your phone) and VantaView fills in the details. Read on the device.
- **Merge channels:** the same channel from several sources becomes one line; the best source plays and the next takes over if it fails.
- **All playlists** in Live TV and the TV Guide, **show or hide channels in bulk**, and **rename playlists**.
- **Protected streams:** ClearKey and Widevine (DASH included) for M3U channels and add-ons. Sports TV also shows add-on rows listed as sports or events, and add-on "other" rows appear on Movies and TV Series.
- **Subtitles:** Croatian, Serbian and Bosnian, plus subtitle add-ons. **Ends at** shows when a film or episode finishes.
- **Add-ons from your phone** with a QR code, and **hold Back in the TV Guide** to go straight back to the last channel.
- **Fixes:** smoother playback, Update now on a TV keeps your place, and picture size changes apply straight away.
- Thanks to u/CrazyDowntown5881 on Reddit for quite a few suggestions and bug reports.

Installs over 2.0.1 with no need to uninstall, and reopens by itself after updating from inside the app.
