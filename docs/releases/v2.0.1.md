VantaView 2.0.1 sets itself up to suit your internet connection.

**Download:** https://github.com/wardy-uk1/VantaView/releases/latest/download/VantaView.apk (always the newest version)

What's new:

- **Connection check in first setup:** a quick speed test (about 25 seconds), then VantaView picks the buffer size, which stream quality to try first, and whether torrents and the Home mini player suit your line.
- **Adjust my settings for this connection:** run it again any time under the speed test in Settings › Sources.
- **Try streams up to this quality first:** a new choice in Settings › Playback (Best, 2160p, 1080p, 720p, 480p). Bigger streams are still tried if nothing else plays.

Installs over 2.0.0 with no need to uninstall, and reopens by itself after updating from inside the app.
