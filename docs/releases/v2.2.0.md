VantaView 2.2.0 brings topic groups for Live TV, more control over which stream plays, other player apps, subtitle timing and smoother playing on phones.

**Download:** https://github.com/wardy-uk1/VantaView/releases/latest/download/VantaView.apk (always the newest version)

What's new:

- **Live TV by topic:** the Groups button can sort channels into News, Sport, Kids and more. **Order › Importance** puts the best-known channels of your countries first (choose your countries in Settings › Channels), and a channel's regional and +1 versions can show as one line. Channel details come from iptv-org's open catalog.
- **Choose your stream:** Settings › Playback › When you press Play. **Show me the streams** opens the list at once and fills it in as add-ons answer. **Play the best stream** starts sooner, skips a link that hasn't started in 15 seconds, and tries very big files last.
- **Other player apps:** send films and series to VLC, MX Player or another player, with subtitles and where you got to (Settings › Playback).
- **Subtitle timing:** a new button in the player moves subtitles earlier or later.
- **Phone player controls:** the top and bottom controls now hide together, and using the top row keeps both up.
- **Smoother on phones:** loading a big playlist or guide no longer competes with the channel you're watching.
- **Fixes:** white writing on light buttons and switches in some looks, and Update now on a TV keeps your place.
- Thanks to u/Turbulent-Invite2673 and u/CrazyDowntown5881 on Reddit for their suggestions and bug reports.

Installs over 2.1.0 with no need to uninstall, and reopens by itself after updating from inside the app.
